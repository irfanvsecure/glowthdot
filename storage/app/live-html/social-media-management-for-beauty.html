- Owner's knowledge hub

Social media questions owners ask, answered straight

How often should a salon post on Instagram?

Consistency beats volume: 4–5 feed posts weekly with near-daily stories outperforms sporadic bursts. The algorithm and the audience both reward rhythm — which is exactly what a managed calendar guarantees.

Do reels really matter for a clinic?

Reels are where non-followers discover you — they're the top of your funnel. Process videos, transformations and POV formats reach cold audiences that static posts never touch. No dancing required.

Does the owner have to be on camera?

Only if you want to. Owner-led content builds connection fast, but treatment content, results and team moments carry accounts perfectly well. We build the format mix around your comfort level.

Organic social or paid ads first?

They compound: organic builds the proof and trust that make ads convert; ads scale the reach organic can't. Most clients start with managed organic, then feed the winners into paid — already proven content, cheaper results.

How do you measure ROI on social media?

By counting the things that pay: profile visits, booking-link taps, DM enquiries and appointments attributed to social. Reach and follower charts are context in our reports — never the headline.

What about TikTok?

TikTok is a discovery amplifier for beauty in the UAE — the same reel-first content strategy extends there naturally. We recommend it once Instagram is running consistently, so quality never dilutes.
