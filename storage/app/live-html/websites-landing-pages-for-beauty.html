Website development services — questions owners ask, answered straight

How much does a salon website cost in the UAE?

Professional builds typically run AED 5,000–25,000 depending on pages, booking integration and content; single landing pages cost far less. We quote fixed after a short scoping call — no hourly surprise

WordPress, Shopify or custom — what do you build on?

WordPress for most salon & clinic sites (you own it, anyone can maintain it), Shopify when retail products matter, custom when performance demands it. The platform serves the goal, never the other way round.

Can you redesign without losing my Google rankings?

Yes — migrations run with full URL mapping and redirects, so earned rankings transfer. Done right, rankings usually improve post-launch because speed and structure improve.

What actually makes visitors book?

Speed, prices, proof and a low-friction path — in that order. Clients bounce from slow sites, distrust hidden pricing, need reviews at the decision moment, and book where WhatsApp is one tap away.

Do I need a website if my Instagram is strong?

Instagram rents you the audience; the website converts and owns it. Google searches, ad clicks and AI recommendations all land somewhere — the businesses capturing them have a fast site with booking built in.

Who writes the content?

Our in-house beauty content specialists do — compliance-aware copy built on the same keyword map used in our SEO services, so every page earns search traffic as well as converting it. You review and approve everything.
