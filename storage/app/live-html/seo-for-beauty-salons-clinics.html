SEO questions owners ask, answered straight

How long does SEO take for a clinic?

Local and Maps improvements typically show in 4–8 weeks; competitive treatment keywords take 3–6 months. Unlike ads, every month compounds — rankings earned keep paying without per-click cost.

SEO or Google Ads — which first?

They're teammates, not rivals: Google Ads buy immediate bookings while SEO builds the free channel underneath. Clinics that run both convert better too — appearing in ads and organic reinforces trust on the results page. Our guide to Google Ads keywords for clinics shows where paid search fits.

What are "money keywords" for beauty?

Terms typed by clients ready to book: treatment + city ("hydrafacial dubai"), "near me" searches, and cost queries ("botox price abu dhabi"). We prioritise these over vanity traffic every time. Have more questions? See our full FAQ.

Why does my Google Business Profile matter so much?

For local beauty searches, the Maps 3-pack appears above regular results — and profile signals (reviews, photos, categories, activity) decide who's in it. It's the highest-leverage asset most clinics neglect.

What is schema markup, in plain words?

Structured data (also called schema markup) is code that labels your content so Google — and increasingly AI engines — understand it: this is a clinic, these are services, these are prices. It can make your pages eligible for richer search results (Google never guarantees them) and helps search and AI engines pull accurate facts about your business.

Why a niche SEO agency instead of a generic SEO company?

Because we're not guessing. As a dedicated beauty SEO agency, we already know which keywords convert in the UAE, what content Google rewards in this industry, and the compliance lines aesthetic clinics can't cross — DHA rules in Dubai, DoH in Abu Dhabi, MOHAP federally — the exact things a generalist SEO company spends your budget learning first.
