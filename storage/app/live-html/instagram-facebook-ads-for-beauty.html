- Owner's knowledge hub

Meta ads questions owners ask, answered straight

Boosting posts vs. running real ads — what's the difference?

Boosting optimises for engagement (likes, comments); Ads Manager campaigns optimise for what you actually want — leads and bookings — with proper audiences, placements, exclusions and tracking. Same money, wildly different outcomes.

What budget do Instagram ads need in Dubai?

Meaningful booking volume usually starts at AED 2,500–5,000/month in ad spend. Beauty cost-per-lead in the UAE typically runs AED 8–25 with proper creative — the simulator above shows the maths for your number.

Do I need new content every month?

Yes — creative fatigue is real and arrives in about three weeks. That's why production is included: a monthly batch of reels and statics, planned before the current winners burn out.

Lead forms, WhatsApp or landing pages — which converts best?

In the UAE beauty market, WhatsApp-first paths usually win on volume and speed; lead forms win for premium consultations needing qualification; landing pages win for high-ticket packages. We route each campaign to its best fit — and test.

How do you measure bookings, not likes?

Pixel + conversions API + DM attribution: every campaign reports conversations started, leads captured and appointments booked, with revenue attached. Reach and likes appear in the appendix, where they belong.

What about Ramadan, wedding season and summer?

They're the calendar's profit peaks — each gets a planned offer, creative set and budget shift weeks in advance. Beauty demand in the UAE is seasonal; your ad account should breathe with it.
